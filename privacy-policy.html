Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026

OakRise Point LLC respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through our website and how we use it.

Information We Collect

When you submit a form, we may collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, and location.
  • Your resume, work history, LinkedIn or website link, job interests, and messages.
  • Company and contact information, hiring needs, job descriptions, and uploaded files from employers.

How We Use Information

We use submitted information to respond to messages, provide recruiting and staffing services, review qualifications, and connect candidates with potential employment opportunities.

How We Share Information

Candidate information may be shared with prospective employers when appropriate for recruiting or staffing purposes. Employer information may be shared with potential candidates when appropriate. We also use service providers, including Google services, to store form responses, files, and email notifications.

We do not sell submitted personal information. We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with the law or protect our rights and users.

Storage and Retention

Form responses and uploaded files are stored using Google services. We keep information only as long as reasonably necessary for recruiting, staffing, communication, and business-record purposes.

Your Choices

You may ask us to review, correct, or delete information you submitted. Contact us using the email address below. We may retain limited information when reasonably necessary for legal or business-record purposes.

Information Security

We take reasonable steps to protect submitted information. However, no online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Policy Changes

We may update this policy when our services or information practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will show the latest update.

Contact Us
